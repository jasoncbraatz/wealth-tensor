## 1 · The find

Paper III asserts one count at three sites. §8's class paragraph opens *"**Adding a free parameter to absorb an objection.** Refused five times across this programme and worth recording as a class, since each instance looked locally reasonable:"* and then enumerates, semicolon-separated, **four**: introducing a scaling constant to rescue the dimensional argument; defining a levy's base so a companion paper's claim came out right; letting λ vary freely rather than in a shaped way; and, as §8.1 now concedes, leaning on an unmeasured φ. §8.1 repeats the number — *"the move this programme has refused five times in other costumes (§8) and should have refused here"* — and §A.2.4 repeats it a third time about Λ. The number is five; the list is four; the list is the only evidence for the number anywhere in the repository.

**`REVIEW-004` already found this and it was never built.** Line 624 of `docs/REVIEW-004-pre-posting-dossier.md`: *"III says 'refused five times' three separate times, and §7 enumerates four. The missing fifth is II's ρ — which cannot be counted, because §4.3 concedes the defence was unsound."* That is the disposal of the missing fifth, decided a day before this repair and adopted here unchanged: ρ is excluded on `REVIEW-004`'s grounds, and the exclusion is stated in this document rather than in the manuscript, because a paragraph explaining which instance the paper declines to count is exactly the process-narration charter §3.3 keeps out of the manuscript. The dossier's own pointer had rotted too — it said §7 and the list is in §8 — and `wealthTensor-34` appended the correction in place on 2026-08-14; nothing further is owed there.

**Why nothing caught it.** The count is a NUMERAL IN PROSE, occurring three times, with no arithmetic behind it anywhere in `scripts/`, `tests/` or `src/`. It is the greppable corollary of the `-34` tell in its plainest form: a load-bearing identifier that lives only in prose is unguarded by construction. Every reflow guard in this repository compares text to text and would have carried "five" across any number of passes without complaint, because nothing in the repository knows that this particular word is supposed to be the length of a list four lines below it.

## 2 · The repair, registered before it was performed

**Registered before the edit: the class has FOUR members, the instance that got through is kept, and the manuscript says which one it was.** This is the ruling recorded on the card and it is adopted. Item four is not a refusal — §8.1's φ move was made, relied on as the paper's answer to its most serious standing objection, and later withdrawn; §8.1 says so in its own words, *"should have refused here."* Dropping the list to three to make the arithmetic clean would delete the one member of the class that cost this programme something, and the sentence's job is to record the class, not to flatter it.

**Amendment to the card's pre-written stanza, made here and made explicit: the repair binds TWO numerals, not one.** The card proposed writing "four" at all three sites. That is false at two of them. §8.1's sentence says *"refused ... times in other costumes (§8) and should have refused here"*, and §8.1's φ move **is** one of §8's four — so "other costumes" is the other three, every one of which was genuinely refused. §A.2.4 says the same of Λ, and Λ is not in the list, so its "other costumes" are the four, of which three were refused. Writing "four" beside the word *refused* at either site would assert that the programme refused a move it concedes two lines later that it made: a fresh defect of exactly the class this document is repairing, introduced by the repair. So the manuscript states the **class count (four) once, in §8, where the list is**, and the **refusal count (three) at the two sites that use the word *refused***, and both numerals are derived by the guard from the same enumeration. The card's ruling is unchanged — four members, item four kept, the one that got through named. Only the arithmetic is made honest at the two remote sites.

**Four anchors, one more than the card's three, all inside the two paragraphs already named.** §8's opening becomes *"Faced four times across this programme and worth recording as a class"*; §8's closing clause becomes *"leaning on an unmeasured φ — three refused, the fourth not"*, which is where the class count and the refusal count are reconciled in the manuscript, in eight words and no hedge. §8.1's and §A.2.4's sentences take "three" for "five" and change in no other way. Every anchor is a span with no internal newline, no heading or horizontal rule moves, and the structure delta is declared empty. **No claim is weakened, no hedge is added, no free parameter is introduced, nothing is absorbed as a caveat, and no section outside §8, §8.1 and §A.2.4 is touched.** G-COACH-3 is evaluated across the pass with `defensive_count.py --against` on a copy of the pre-edit file taken before the tree was dirtied, and the full suite is run after.
