## 2 · The repair, registered before it was performed

**Registered before the edit:** the reconciliation exists, it is strong, and it is simply absent from the paper, so the repair is a **steelman and not a caveat**. A recognised impairment is the moment degradation *becomes* estimable; it is the end of the accumulation §2 describes, not an instance of the estimable-loss regime §10 hands to conservatism. §5's sample therefore sits on the **boundary** of §10's restricted region rather than inside its complement, and that boundary is precisely what makes a timeliness interval measurable at all: an unrecognised gap leaves no filing and no date to measure to. Stated positively this **strengthens** §5 — it says why this sample and no other can test §2. **One sentence is added to §5 and §10 is not touched.** No claim is weakened, no hedge is added, no free parameter is introduced, and the objection is *not* pasted into the manuscript as a caveat — the ABSORB move charter §2 forbids. **What this does not do.** It does not widen §2's claim, and it does not license the reading that §2 predicts anything about estimable losses — §10's restriction stands exactly as written, which is why the sentence goes in §5. It does not touch the 1,047 discarded charges or the drop accounting in §11; the sample is unchanged and no number in §5 moves.
