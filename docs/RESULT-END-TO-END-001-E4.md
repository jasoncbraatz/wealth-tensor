## 1 · What was under test, and how this run was constrained

E4's check, quoted whole:

> *"Enumerate every claim in the corpus about **the world** — not about a model, not about a
> literature — that any paper asserts as **confirmed**. Count them."*

> **FAILURE (as an audit finding) is shown by:** the count being zero **and** no document in the
> corpus stating that at corpus level.
> **REFUTED BY:** a positive count with the claims named, or a paper that already states the
> corpus-level position in terms.

Truth-functionally, with `Z` = the count is zero and `S` = the corpus-level position is stated:
**FAILURE = `Z ∧ ¬S`**; **REFUTED = `¬Z ∨ S`**. These are complements on those two limbs, so the
first disjunct alone suffices and no ordering is implied by the "or". **E4 is not `E5`** — `E5` died
because its failure limb was a disjunction and its refutation limb a conjunction of the same two
limbs, leaving a gap in the middle. E4's clause pair does have two reachable gaps, but the corpus
does not sit in either; they are recorded in §5.3 and routed to `END-TO-END-002`, not repaired here.

**The corpus is Papers II, III and IV** (§1). `papers/paper-I-price-formation/paper-I.md` exists,
carries the same *"no empirical data is used at all"* disclosure, and was **excluded by scope rather
than overlooked**.

**Exclusions applied, exactly the two the registration names and no third.** Model results are out
(Paper II §3 entire; Paper III §§2–3, §4's theorem and its corollaries, §A.2's sweeps; Paper IV §5's
25-allocation identity and §4's SMD resolution). Literature results are out — including **Paper
IV §6 / `REG-013` in its entirety**, the co-citation ceiling 0.477, the floor of 0.000 and the three
overlaps, on the registration's own gloss that a citation graph is *"a fact about what a literature
reads, not about what anyone measures."*

**No third exclusion was added.** The obvious one — *"and not about accounting recognition
practice"* — would have zeroed the count, and this run declines to write it, for two reasons. First,
§0 fixes the document (*"a test designed after the results are in is not a severe test"*), and
adding an exclusion after seeing the count is the mirror image of adding an observable to a leg's
own failure criterion, which `-57` forbade and which binds. Second, **the registration itself
already drew this line, the other way, nine lines earlier**: E2's power check reads *"Paper II
measures a model class. **Paper III measures a filter and one EDGAR sample.** `REG-013` measured a
citation graph"* — and its own candidate sentence is classified as *"a claim about **the world's
measurement practice**."* The designer put recording practice on the world side in the same fixed
document. This run is bound by that and not by its own convenience.

**Method.** Two independent extractions over the three manuscripts, then one reading dedicated to
the second disjunct, then **two rounds of refutation** — round one against the finding this run
built, round two aimed at the item round one could not kill and at the four assertions round one

## 5 · Defects in the instrument, found while running it

### 5.1 · The registration is now **0-for-8**, and the eighth is the seventh's twin

`END-TO-END-001` §E6, quoting Paper IV §9.2:

> *"Diagonality at the firm scale is assumed and **its test is open**. **If** recognition events
> cluster within firm-quarters, the Hadamard form in §3 is wrong and the chain has a broken link."*

The source, `paper-IV.md.bak-wt54-liftoff` lines 513–515:

> *"…the Hadamard form in §3 is wrong and the chain has a broken link **at the scale where
> accounting happens**."*

**The trailing scope clause is dropped, with no ellipsis.** That is the seventh defect's exact
species — `-59` found the registration quoting the corpus without §4.3's trailing clause and wrote
the rule *"do not quote a sentence into a finding without its trailing scope clause"* four hours
before this run found the same document doing it again, one leg over, against the same paper. **The
first six were the document being unreliable about itself. It is now twice unreliable about the
corpus, and both times by amputating a qualifier.**

**A ninth was proposed by this run's own refuter and is REJECTED, deliberately and on the record.**
§3's *"Paper III §6.1 governs the corpus as it governs Paper III: the framework has no confirmed
empirical claim"* is not falsified by a positive count. *"Governs"* is a scope-transfer claim about
which standard applies, the colon-clause is a paraphrase of §6.1 rather than an independent
assertion, and the claim it would need to contradict is about **the framework**, whose one
disconfirmed prediction the count does not touch. **A defect tally that accepts every candidate is
worth nothing**, and this one is easier to inflate than to keep honest.

### 5.2 · E4's own clause pair has two reachable gaps — `END-TO-END-002`, not a repair here

FAILURE requires *"no document in the corpus stating that **at corpus level**"*; REFUTED requires
*"a paper that already states the corpus-level position **in terms**"*, and a positive count *"**with
the claims named**."* Two states satisfy neither clause:

- **the strictness gap** — a paper stating the corpus-level position by clear implication but not
  *in terms* defeats FAILURE's second conjunct and does not satisfy REFUTED's second disjunct;
- **the naming gap** — a positive count whose claims are **not named** defeats both.

E4, like E5, declares no UNDECIDED region although §0' promises one *"where one applies"*. **Same
failure mode as E5, at a different joint** — E5's was a predicate mismatch, E4's are a strictness
mismatch and a conjunct mismatch. **The `document` / `paper` asymmetry is NOT one of them**: §1
defines the corpus as exactly Papers II, III and IV, so the two terms are coextensive and the
scenario cannot be instantiated. Reported because this run's own refuter proposed it and it took
reading §1 to close it.

### 5.3 · E4's remedy is stated unconditionally, and that is a design defect

Every other leg conditions its remedy on an outcome. E4's reads *"**What the corpus does —
pre-registered, so the run cannot argue about it:** the remedy is **one sentence in Paper IV** … "*
with no antecedent. **This run does not apply it.** `-57`'s ruling binds and `-59` applied it to E2:
a remedy whose triggering finding did not occur may not be applied, because applying it is
re-choosing a fixed clause in the direction the run prefers. **The ambiguity is flagged, not
resolved** — resolving it needs `END-TO-END-002`, and a successor who thinks the sentence should be
written anyway should note that the sentence E4 specifies (*"what the corpus has and has not
established about the world"*) is now a **harder** sentence to write than the designer expected,
because the true answer is five claims and a stale summary rather than a clean zero.

## 0' · The tell this leg adds

**AN AUDIT LEG'S EXPECTED ANSWER IS AN INSTRUMENT READING TAKEN BEFORE THE INSTRUMENT WAS BUILT.**
E4 was classified AUDIT *"because the designer expects the count to be zero"* — and the designer had
Paper III §9's ninth limitation open while writing, quoted *"4.12× and 2.02×"* verbatim into E6 in
the same document, and still expected zero. The expectation was not lazy; it was **inherited from
the corpus's own summary sentence**, which had been stale for two weeks. **A designer who quotes a
paper's self-assessment into a leg's premises has imported that paper's staleness into his own
instrument** — and the only thing that catches it is running the leg he told you the answer to.
*Classifying a leg AUDIT protects the run from reporting a discovery. It does not protect the
designer from being wrong, and this is the first leg of the pass where those came apart.*
