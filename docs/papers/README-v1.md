corpus's own end-to-end check (`docs/RESULT-END-TO-END-001-E1.md`) demoted that to "one question
asked three times". Its three surviving parts — the SMD-as-boundary paragraph, the crossing-height
instance, and the pre-registered citation-whitespace instrument — are worth redistributing rather
than carrying a paper to hold them.
