## 4 · FINDINGS RAISED AND REPAIRED, BY SEVERITY

**Seventy findings across sixteen passes, scored retrospectively at Pass A against a rubric
committed before the scoring began** (`DoD` § 1.3, and the point of committing it first).

| severity | count | share | disposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| **S1** — the manuscript states something not true | **53** | 75.7 % | all repaired; **zero open** |
| **S2** — the manuscript asserts something nothing supports | **16** | 22.9 % | all repaired; **zero open** |
| **S3** — precision, taste, honest imperfection | **1** | 1.4 % | **ships, named in § 6** |

**MEASUREMENT ANSWERED — the last open question on the post-ship list.** *"Was severity falling
while the count stayed flat?"* — **NO, and neither was.** The last six passes returned nineteen S1s
against the first five passes' twenty-one, on a corpus already repaired forty-five times. Severity
did not decay; the passes kept finding true defects at a steady rate to the end. **That is the
finding that justified replacing a convergence criterion with a punch list**, and it is why the old
stopping rule had no termination proof.

**AND THE S3 TAIL EVERYONE ASSUMED WAS ACCUMULATING DOES NOT EXIST: one S3 in seventy findings.**
The apparatus was never producing taste; it was producing truth defects, which is a different
statement about the corpus than anyone expected to be able to make.

**Nine further S1s were found after the freeze — three by Pass C, six by Pass D — and every one was
repaired in the session that found it.** Per § 1.2's successor rule they did not reopen
`SHIP-LIST.md`; the blocking set never grew. **Five of Pass D's six came from reading the three
manuscripts against each other**, which no earlier pass had done.

## 6 · WHAT IS KNOWN-IMPERFECT AND SHIPPING ANYWAY

**This is the section § 4.2 exists for.** Nothing here blocks; everything here is disclosed in the
manuscripts themselves as well as recorded here.

**6.1 · The two S3s ship, named in the papers.** paper-II § 3.1's *"4–7 %"* band is an under-claim
— the sweep's largest residual is 6.831 %, so the band is true and looser than the evidence
requires. And § 1's characterisation of zakat has no primary source; the paper says so, and says
its argument does not depend on it. **Both are now in paper-II's own § 4.4 note, not only here.**

**6.2 · Register drift is flagged and not fixed, at ten sites.** `REVIEW-040` § 2 locates each one.
**This is a deliberate refusal**: re-voicing is the author's pass, and a session that harmonises
the voice has spent his hours for him and probably lost something. Two of the ten (paper-IV § 8 and
§ 10) are good writing in their own register and are flagged because they *differ*, not because
they are wrong.

**6.3 · Fifty-three soft apparatus references are named without a gloss.** A `scripts/wt###.py` or
a `REG-0NN` code in a data-and-code section is a committed, fetchable artefact beside a public
repository — it is the provenance promise the whole apparatus exists to keep, and stripping it
would delete the corpus's best feature. A first-time reader meeting one mid-sentence may still
stumble. **The repair is one clause at first use and it was not taken**; § 4.1 permits it and does
not require it.

**6.4 · One structural judgement call stands, and it is the author's to overrule.** paper-III's § 4
leans forward onto § 5.4's empirical fit. Pass C repaired this by moving **definitions** forward to
the point of use rather than by moving sections, on the reasoning that § 4 is the identification
theory and § 5 the severe test. Pass D read § 4 at thirty thousand feet and concurs. **If the
dependency still costs a reader, that is a section move and it belongs to a ruling, not to a fifth
session doing it quietly.**

**6.5 · The framework has no confirmed empirical claim, and paper-III says so in its own § 6.1.**
Two pre-registered severe tests were run and both were lost. **That is a result, reported at full
length in the body and in the abstract, not filed under abandonments.** It is the single most
important thing in this corpus for a reader to know, and no part of the review apparatus makes it
smaller.

**6.6 · What no instrument here measures.** Nothing in this repository measures where a reader
**first meets** a value — Pass C discovered that a fold repaired a hundred lines below the table
that introduces the term leaves the fold, with every checker green. Nothing measures cross-manuscript
consistency either; Pass D found nine such defects by reading, and the sweep that found them was a
one-off, not an instrument. **Both are named in `POST-SHIP.md` as instruments worth building, and
both are blocked by § 1.1 until the corpus has shipped — which, as of this file, it has.**

## 8 · WHAT SHIPPING DOES NOT MEAN

**It does not mean the corpus is right.** It means every claim in it is either regenerated by a
named command, checked against a named source, or disclosed as neither. The severe test failed.
The framework's empirical content is one registered prediction that lost. **A reader who wants to
know what this corpus has established about the world should read paper-III § 6.1 literally, and
this file does not soften it.**
