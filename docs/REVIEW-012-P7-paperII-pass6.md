## 1 · Method, and the first coverage claim in this project you can put a number on

This pass ran the **quantifier sweep** (`WT-115`) — the method `-71` produced and nobody had yet
run on a whole manuscript — plus a targeted **blast-radius audit of `wt128`**, `-71`'s own repairs.

The sweep is now an instrument rather than a habit: **`scripts/wt130_quantifier_sweep.py`**
enumerates every quantifier token in a manuscript with its line number, and it runs on any of the
four. That matters more than this pass's findings do. *"I read it carefully"* is a narrative
coverage claim and cannot be diffed; **`155 quantifier tokens on 117 lines`** can be, and the next
pass can regenerate it and see what moved.

| manuscript | lines | quantifier tokens |
|---|---|---|
| paper-I-price-formation | 138 | 184 |
| **paper-II-redistribution** | **117** (118 after repair) | **155** (155 after repair) |
| paper-III-dual-tensor | 668 | 864 |
| paper-IV-composition | 153 | 194 |

**The procedure, for each of the 117 lines:** read **FORWARD** to the end of the document and ask
one question — *does anything below this sentence belong to the set it just counted, and is it in
it?* A quantifier is written while looking at the material **above** it; the set it ranges over is
finished **below** it. That is why a census (what does the text say), an identity guard (did this
body survive) and a positional criterion (where does this item sit) are all structurally incapable
of catching one: **a quantifier defect is a claim about a set, and nothing in the guard kit
enumerates a set.** Three of the 117 fell. All three had survived five prior passes.

Token total is unchanged at 155 across the repair — reported as measured, not as a derivation.

## 2 · FINDINGS — three, all repaired in-pass, none carded

### `II-19` · §1 contribution 5 makes the exact claim §7 forbids by name

**§1, contribution 5:** *"every number below is regenerated by **a single command** from a public
repository"*.

**§7, four sections down:** *"Regenerate every number in §3: `python3 scripts/wt030_report.py` —
**except** the three Var[log *a*] values in §3.1 … come from `python3 scripts/wt077_tail_index.py`.
The two commands are named separately because **a single command named for numbers it does not
produce is a provenance claim that reads as checked and is not.**"*

**Named falsifier:** §7's two-command bullet. This is the sharpest instance of the species the
project has found, because §7 does not merely contradict §1 — it *names the failure mode §1 has
committed*, and the two sentences have coexisted through five reviews. `REVIEW-005` §117 even
quotes §7's warning approvingly while §1 sat four hundred lines above it, unread against it.

**Repair (no new number):** §1 now reads *"regenerated from a public repository by the two commands
§7 names"*. §7 unchanged.

### `II-20` · §7 contradicts itself four lines later — and this is `wt128`'s blast radius

**§7, opening:** *"no empirical data is used at all — **every number is generated by simulation**."*
**§7, four lines later:** the three Var[log *a*] values are *"quadrature over the multiplier's
distribution **rather than simulation output**"*.

**Named falsifier:** §7's own next bullet. **`wt128` (‑71) propagated exactly this carve-out into
§5 limitation 5** — *"every **simulated** number above … the exception is §3.1's three Var[log *a*]
values"* — and did not read §7's own universal against it. The brief said to look for `wt128`'s
blast radius outside its four sections; this is it, and it is the only hit (§4 below records the
rest of that audit).

**Repair, and why it is an APPEND and not a rewrite:** the clause *"no empirical data is used at
all — every number is generated by simulation"* is **quoted verbatim** in `docs/END-TO-END-001.md`
(L282) and `docs/RESULT-END-TO-END-001-E3.md` (L313). End-to-end is **CLOSED** and its quoted text
is a dated record, so the repair extends the sentence rather than rewriting it — *"…by simulation,
**save the three quadrature values the next bullet names**"* — and `wt129` carries a **substring
guard** asserting the quoted clause survives literally. Both records remain true quotations of a
substring; neither was touched. Precedent: `patch_wt56_e1_remedy.py`'s quoted text, left alone on
purpose.

### `II-21` · §2.3's "no result below depends on a value of ρ we picked" is falsified by §3.1 and §3.2

**§2.3:** *"it is **swept rather than chosen**: **no result below depends on a value of ρ we
picked**, and the paper's central result is a statement about the whole ρ axis."*

**Named falsifier:** §3.1's table footnote — *"The flow rows are assessed at full realisation,
ρ = 1 — §2.3's mark-to-market case and **the implementation's default**"* — read against §3.2's
table, which shows the reachable flow Gini running **0.125 → 0.395 → 0.994** as ρ goes 1.00 → 0.25
→ 0.00. §3.1's headline frontier result, *"the reachable frontiers are stock 0.000 < flow 0.125"*,
**is the ρ = 1 number**; at ρ = 0 the flow frontier is 0.994 and there is no nesting at all. Every
flow row in §3.1 depends on a picked ρ, and §3.2 is the proof.

**This is the honest reading, not a pedantic one.** The sentence's defensible content is *ρ is not
a free parameter fitted to rescue a claim* — which is true and worth saying. What it actually
asserts is stronger and false.

**Repair (no new number):** §2.3 now reads *"§3.1's flow rows are stated at ρ = 1 and labelled as
such, §3.2 sweeps the axis, and the paper's central result is a statement about the whole ρ axis
rather than about a value of ρ chosen to make it come out right."* The defensible half survives; the
universal is gone; the disclosure now points at the two sections that carry it.

**Deliberately NOT changed:** the abstract's *"the frontiers are nested, stock 0.000 against flow
0.125"* carries the same ρ = 1 dependency undisclosed. The abstract is **244 words / 1478
characters**, the closest to arXiv's ceiling of any manuscript in the batch, `P3l` requires
`falsified` **and** `nested` to survive in it, and §3.1's footnote discloses ρ = 1 one screen below.
`wt129` asserts the abstract is **byte-identical**. If a later pass judges the abstract must carry
it, that is a word-budget decision, not a review finding.
