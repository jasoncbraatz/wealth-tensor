## The three-sentence version

Sixteen review passes ran across thirty-one sessions under a criterion — *"repeat until two
consecutive passes yield zero findings"* — that **none of them ever satisfied**, because the backlog
was stationary and the rule also rewarded reading carelessly. A frozen punch list replaced it at
`-102` and **closed the same work in four passes**. The referees were never the problem: of seventy
findings, **fifty-three were outright falsehoods and exactly one was a nitpick**, and the severity
never decayed from pass one to pass sixteen.

## What a future wealth-tensor session should take from it

- **`docs/POST-SHIP.md` holds 29 deferred items and nobody has scored them.** That is action A5 on
  the AAR. An unscored deferred backlog is how a second convergence loop starts.
- **The rubric in `DEFINITION-OF-DONE-SHIP.md` §2 + §2.5 is the good artefact here** — truth *and*
  coherence, as two instruments rather than one. If this corpus reopens after Jason's v1.0 ruling,
  re-judge against **both**, one pass each, and emit a punch list. Do not reopen an unbounded pass.
- **`docs/p7-passes.tsv` is the reason this retrospective is measurement and not anecdote.** It was
  built at `-76` specifically because six handoffs had asserted something nobody had measured, and
  it went on to refute its own project's favourite explanation four times. **Build the ledger that
  can prove you wrong** — it is the single most valuable file in this repository that is not a
  manuscript.
