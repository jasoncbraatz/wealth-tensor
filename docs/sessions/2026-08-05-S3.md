## What went wrong, and what it cost

**One real design error, mine.** PRE-001's onset rule keyed on an *unbroken* run of YoY revenue
declines. That measures the volatility of the signal rather than the phenomenon, and it cost the
first of two runs. The registration anticipated the direction of the problem (§8.4, "costs power
rather than bias") and underestimated its size by roughly a factor of four.

It was caught by the drop accounting the registration required — 1,047 discards and zero
censoring were visible before any theory was invoked. **The registration's own honesty
requirements diagnosed the registration.** That is the argument for writing them down.

**Two smaller ones.** A test fixture built a 181-day period and called it a quarter, which looked
like a parser bug for a minute and was a fixture bug. And `dx --put` fails with a bare "no such
file or directory" when the parent directory is missing on darwin, which reads as a transport
fault (L20).
