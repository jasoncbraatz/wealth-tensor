## 1 · The finding behind the findings: a narrowing repairs one site and breaks every site that agreed with it

`-61`'s tell — *a corpus under repair has a moving referent* — has been read four sessions running
in its **missed-defect** direction: diff before you read, or you will review a document that has
moved. This pass met it in the other direction, and the other direction is worse.

`wt121` was a **correct, ruled, well-guarded** patch. It changed the abstract's framing from a
ladder to type identity. Every other passage in Paper IV that had been written to **agree** with
the old abstract was, at the instant that patch landed, **newly wrong** — not by having changed,
but by having stayed. `-63`'s `REVIEW-006` read §1 and passed it, and `REVIEW-006` was right at the
time: §1's thesis sentence and the old abstract said the same thing in the same shape.

**A framing patch's blast radius is not the lines it edits; it is every line that quoted the old
framing in its own words.** The census `WT-099` demands catches the *strings*. Nothing in the kit
catches the *paraphrases*, and the paraphrase in this case was the sentence the paper labels its
own central claim.

## 2 · The findings

### `IV-11` · §1 ¶3 — the paper's own thesis sentence still walks the ladder · **REPAIRED**

Before, at L54–56, thirty-five lines below a narrowed abstract:

> This paper is the claim that they are layers of one stack, and that the object at the bottom of it
> composes: **the same atomic unit, a household's, aggregates to a firm's and to a sovereign's
> without changing type.**

`WT-102`'s stated reasoning applies here **with more force than at the site it was written for**:
*"nobody reads 'from X to the Y' as type identity; they read it as a ladder."* **"Aggregates to A
and to B" is a verb of derivation** — a stronger ladder than the "from…to" that was removed. And
the trailing *"without changing type"* is precisely the defence `WT-102` classified as *"a retreat
even when it is correct"*, now embedded in the sentence rather than offered afterwards.

The abstract and the introduction are the two things a referee reads in the first two minutes, and
after `wt121` they disagreed about what the paper claims.

After:

> This paper is the claim that they are layers of one stack, and that the object at the bottom of
> it has one type wherever it appears: **a household's holding, a firm's balance sheet and a
> sovereign's accounts are the same kind of object, and summing holdings does not change the
> kind.**

The three scales are now **named flat**, mirroring the narrowed abstract's *"one type at the
household, firm and sovereign scales"*. Nothing was conceded: the addition mechanism survives as
*"summing holdings does not change the kind"*, which is §2.2's definitional claim, and the next
sentence's *"a nearby and stronger version of it is false"* still does its work.

### `IV-12` · §3 opens with a joiner its own closing forbids · **REPAIRED**

§3's opening claim, L167–169:

> The claim is that **the same object appears at each scale and the operator that moves between
> them is addition**.

§3's closing, fifty lines later, L217–219:

> So what joins the scales is the question and the fact that each scale answers it quantitatively,
> which is what this section now claims **and no more**.

**Addition is a joiner.** Read straight through, the section's closing deletes its own opening, and
a referee cannot tell which sentence is the paper's position. The closing is the over-reaching one:
`END-TO-END-001` leg `E1` removed the **chain** — ρ and φ as one object seen twice — and never
touched §2.2's addition, which is definitional and was never at risk.

Repaired in the closing rather than the opening, because the opening is the true claim:

> question, the fact that each scale answers it quantitatively, and the addition of §2.2 — which is
> what this section now claims and no more.

"No more" is now true as written, and the §2.2 pointer makes the exemption checkable rather than
implied.

### `IV-13` · §3's two scale transitions assert **nesting**, which is both false and the disclaimed structure · **REPAIRED, two edits**

`IV-13a`, §3 **Firm**, before:

> **Firm.** A balance sheet is the household's holding, summed and reported.

`IV-13b`, §3 **Sovereign**, before:

> **Sovereign.** National accounts are firms summed…

Both are written as **part-whole identities**: a firm *is* households summed; a sovereign *is*
firms summed. Two problems, and the first is the substantive one.

**They are false as set composition.** Households are not constituents of firms — a firm's balance
sheet is the *firm's* holdings summed, and no quantity of households adds up to a corporation.
National accounts are not firms summed either: the SNA's institutional sectors include households,
general government and NPISH alongside corporations. **The paper contradicts itself on this within
§3**: its own **Household** paragraph is a household holding, which under "national accounts are
firms summed" could only reach the sovereign scale by first becoming a firm.

**And nesting is exactly the "one structure" the narrowed abstract now disclaims.** The framework's
claim is one *type* at three *named* scales plus addition **within** a scale. Writing the
transitions as containment re-asserts the ladder in its strongest available form — stronger than
the abstract's old wording, because containment is a claim about *membership*, not about sequence.

After:

> **Firm.** A balance sheet is holdings of the household's kind, summed and reported.

> **Sovereign.** National accounts are the same holdings summed across every institutional sector,
> households and firms alike…

Type identity where type identity was meant; the household sector restored to the sovereign scale
directly, where the national accounts actually put it.

## 4 · The instrument lesson: a count-based guard names the wrong lines

`wt125`'s first `--dry` failed on the character-width guard. Correct behaviour — ED2's replacement
had re-joined a sentence into a 120-character line. But the guard was written (copied from `wt124`)
as `assert len(wide) <= len(old_wide)`, and its failure text therefore printed **six lines: the one
introduced and five pre-existing** front-matter lines that had been over 100 characters since the
paper was created. The session had to diff two lists by eye to find which line was its own.

**A guard that reports a COUNT makes you find the defect yourself; a guard that reports a SET
hands it to you.** Changed in `wt125` to `assert wide <= old_wide` over sets, failing with
`sorted(wide - old_wide)` — exactly the introduced lines and nothing else. On the second `--dry`
the same five pre-existing lines print as informational output, clearly separated from a pass.

This is the same species as `-66b`'s *"a census reports the historical category separately rather
than dropping it silently"* — an instrument must distinguish **what it found** from **what was
already there**, or its output is a diffing exercise wearing a guard's clothes.
